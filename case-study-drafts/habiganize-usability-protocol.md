# Habiganize usability test — facilitator script + notes

**Download the note sheet:** [habiganize-usability-notesheet.pdf](./habiganize-usability-notesheet.pdf) (print 5 copies, one per participant). Fillable HTML: [habiganize-usability-notesheet.html](./habiganize-usability-notesheet.html).

**Product:** https://habitganizer.tech (web, English, incognito, logged out)  
**n = 5 · 25 minutes + 5 minute buffer · fresh account each person**  
**Flows:** signup → add habit → Today check-in → discover rewards → adopt a pet → care  

Do not say until they say them: Pups, Shop, coins, food, water, Care Loop, decay, streak, Clerk, tab names.

If they stall 15 seconds: “What are you trying to do?”  
If they stall another 15: mark **A** (assisted) and give the smallest hint.

---

## Research questions

1. Can a first-time user get from empty account to a completed habit without coaching?
2. Do they notice the reward, and do they know what it is for?
3. Can they adopt a pet and care for it as one job?
4. Does skipping a day feel like care or like punishment?
5. Is signup a wall?

## Recruit

**Must:** tried a habit/fitness/streak app; quit or felt guilty after missing a day; has not used Habiganize; does not know you built it.  
**Skip:** current users, people who have seen demos, UX researchers who will “perform” the test.  
**Nice:** 20s–30s, 2–3 prior apps. Mix one phone session if you can.

## Product facts (you, not them)

- Habits are created on **Habits** (Plus). Empty Today tells them to add habits there.
- Check-in pays **+10 coins, +1 food, +1 water**.
- Cheapest pet is **Toby / Shiba = 50 coins** (five check-ins).
- Do **not** pre-seed the wallet. If they are short in S5, say they can add a few more small things they might do today and check those off.
- Pilot S1–S6 yourself on a throwaway account once before participant 1.

## Session clock

| Min   | Block            | You do                                      |
| ----- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 0–2   | Consent + rules  | Think-aloud. No right answer. You stay quiet. |
| 2–5   | Background       | Last app, why they stopped. Product still closed. |
| 5–8   | S1 Get in        | Open the URL. They sign up.                 |
| 8–13  | S2 + S3          | Add one real habit. Log it as done today.   |
| 13–16 | S4 Discover loop | Unprompted: what can you do with what you earned? |
| 16–21 | S5 + S6          | Get a companion. Take care of it.           |
| 21–27 | Debrief          | Five questions. Thank them.                 |

## Opening script (verbatim)

> Thanks for doing this. I’m testing the app, not you. There are no right answers. Please think out loud — say what you’re looking at, what you expect to happen, and if something feels weird. I may go quiet on purpose. You can stop at any time. I’ll record the screen and audio so I don’t miss things. Is that okay?

If yes, start recording, then:

> I’ll give you short situations. Do whatever you would do if you were actually trying this for a week.

---

## Background (product closed)

- Last habit or streak app? How long did they last?
- Why did they stop?
- How do they feel about streaks?
- What would make them open an app tomorrow?

---

## Scenarios (read verbatim)

**S1 — Get in**  
“You heard about a small habit app and you want to try it for a week. Get yourself set up so you can start tracking something today.”  
Success: signed-in main screen. Fail: abandon signup / cannot tell if they are in.

**S2 — Add a real habit**  
“You want to drink more water this week. Set that up in the app in whatever way makes sense to you.”  
Success: one habit exists and they can see it. Partial: created but missing on Today.

**S3 — Check in**  
“You just drank a glass of water. Log that you did it today.”  
Success: marked complete. If they do not mention a reward, ask once: “Did anything happen when you checked that off?”

**S4 — Discover the loop (do not name the tab)**  
“You earned something. Figure out what it is for, and what this app wants you to do next. Talk through what you are looking at.”  
Success: they say check-ins produce resources **and** find where those are spent.

**S5 — Get a companion**  
“You want something in the app to take care of, not just a list. Get one.”  
Success: they own a pet. Coin-short bridge: “You can add a few more small things you might actually do today, and check those off too.”

**S6 — Care**  
“Your companion looks like it needs something. Take care of it the way the app seems to want.”  
Success: feed, water, or another care action, and they can point to a meter that changed.

---

## Debrief (this order)

1. In your own words, what is this app?
2. What would make you open it tomorrow morning?
3. Imagine you skip tomorrow. What do you think happens? How does that feel?
4. Was anything confusing, annoying, or hard to tap or read?
5. Would you keep the companion you picked? Why or why not?

After they leave (10 min, same day): (1) could they complete the loop, (2) one quote, (3) one change you would make.

## Codes (tick only these)

| Code  | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| HES   | Paused >5s |
| BACK  | Backtracked / switched tabs without finishing |
| IA    | Looked in the wrong place |
| MISS  | Reward/toast/meter appeared; they missed it |
| LOOP+ | Said habits fund/feed the pet in their words |
| LOOP- | Pets as decoration or a separate game |
| CARE  | Pet as something that needs them |
| GUILT | Skip-day = failure / streak shame |
| AUTH  | Signup was the hard part |
| A11Y  | Contrast, tiny target, hard to read, overload |
| ASK   | Asked you what to do |

**Result:** C complete · CS struggle · F fail · A assisted  
**SEQ:** 1 very hard – 7 very easy  
**Severity (after session):** 0 cosmetic · 1 minor · 2 major · 3 critical · 4 blocker
